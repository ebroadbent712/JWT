# Just Wear This client questionnaire

Clients answer six questions (about three minutes). The photographer adds the session details she already knows. Answers can arrive in any format: pasted from Google Forms, a client system like HoneyBook or Dubsado, an email or a text. Read them as written; never require a specific format.

## Photographer adds (from the booking)

- Session type (family, extended family, couple, maternity)
- Date (month is enough)
- Location and setting
- Time of day
- Indoor or outdoor

If any of these are missing, ask the photographer in one short message. Never ask the client.

## Client questions

**1. Who's coming?** For each person:
- First name or role
- Age group: Adult · Teen · Kid (with age) · Toddler · Baby
- Shop from: Women's · Men's · Girls' · Boys' · Either
- Dressing up: Happy to · Basics only (jeans and a nice top)
- Anything they won't wear (optional)

**2. How dressy?** Relaxed · Dressy casual · Polished · Formal · Not sure, you decide

**3. Colors you love, or want to avoid?** (optional)

**4. Prints?** Love them · A little is nice · Solids please

**5. Anything you already own and want to wear?** Photos plus who it's for; optionally a photo of the room where the photos will hang.

**6. Anything else?** Comfort needs, pregnancy, mobility or sensory needs, modesty, worries.

## How answers map to the plan

| Answer | Use |
| --- | --- |
| People, ages, shop from | Board labels, layout order (adults first, then kids oldest to youngest), which library pieces fit |
| "Basics only" | The won't-dress-up rule |
| Won't wear | Hard no's (highest priority) |
| Dress level | `dress_level` in the plan, shown on the board; "Not sure" → choose from the setting (usually Dressy casual) |
| Colors | Color story choice; avoided colors are never used |
| Prints | "Solids please" → no prints, texture carries interest; "Love them" → pattern mixing allowed within the rules |
| Owned pieces | `owned` list; build around them |
| Room photo | Weigh the room's colors when choosing the story |
| Anything else | Sensitive-case rules |

No sizes and no budget: clients choose sizes and price tiers on the shop links.
